What is VLSI?

VLSI (Very Large Scale Integration) is the process of designing and manufacturing integrated circuits by combining millions of transistors on a single chip.

VLSI Design Flow

  1. Specification
  2. Architecture Design
  3. RTL Design
  4. Functional Verification
  5. Synthesis
  6. Floor Planning
  7. Placement
  8. Clock Tree Synthesis
  9. Routing
  10. Physical Verification
  11. Fabrication

CMOS Technology

CMOS (Complementary Metal Oxide Semiconductor) technology is widely used for designing low-power and high-performance integrated circuits.

CMOS Advantages

  • Low Power Consumption
  • High Noise Immunity
  • High Integration Density
  • High Reliability
